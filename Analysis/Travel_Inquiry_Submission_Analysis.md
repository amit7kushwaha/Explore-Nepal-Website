

## 1. Purpose
Provide a secure, easy-to-use contact and inquiry form so website visitors can ask questions about Nepal travel packages, itineraries, and custom trips.

## 2. User Requirements
- Visitors can fill out an inquiry form with their Name, Email Address, Travel Destination/Topic, and Question/Message.
- Required fields (Name, Email, Message) must be validated before submitting so incomplete forms are not sent.
- Visitors receive a clear confirmation message after submitting their inquiry.
- No login is required to submit a general travel inquiry.
