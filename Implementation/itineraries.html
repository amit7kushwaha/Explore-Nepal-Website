3-Day Pokhara Scenic Getaway

Region: Gandaki Province | Duration: 3 Days

Experience the serene lakes, mountain views, and adventure sports of Pokhara.

Day 1: Arrival in Pokhara, Phewa Lake boating, and Lakeside stroll.

Day 2: Early morning sunrise at Sarangkot, visit World Peace Pagoda.

Day 3: Davis Falls, Gupteshwor Cave, and departure.

5-Day Kathmandu Cultural & Heritage Tour

Region: Kathmandu Valley | Duration: 5 Days

Immerse yourself in ancient temples, rich history, and Newari culture.

Day 1: Arrival in Kathmandu, hotel check-in, and Thamel evening walk.

Day 2: Visit Kathmandu Durbar Square and Swayambhunath Stupa.

Day 3: Explore Pashupatinath Temple and Boudhanath Stupa.

Day 4: Day trip to ancient Bhaktapur Durbar Square and Patan.

Day 5: Souvenir shopping and departure.
